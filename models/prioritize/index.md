# Prioritize & Allocate

*Scarce time and resources — decide what deserves focus, money, and effort.*

**Entry question:** What should get my attention and resources?

Return to the [Decision jobs](/index.md) map or the [models hub](/models/index.md).

## Models

* [BCG Matrix](bcg-matrix.md) - How to evaluate cost versus expected benefit across efforts that compete for your resources.
* [Eisenhower Matrix](eisenhower-matrix.md) - How to learn to work more efficiently by sorting tasks on urgency vs importance.
* [Pareto Principle](pareto-principle.md) - Why you get 80% of results from 20% of effort — unequal distributions in causes and outcomes.
* [Project Management Triangle](project-management-triangle.md) - Why perfection does not exist — you cannot maximize scope, time, and cost at once.
* [Project Portfolio](project-portfolio.md) - How to keep an overview of your projects so nothing important stays invisible.
* [SMART Model](smart-model.md) - How to check whether you are pursuing the right goal by making it specific, measurable, and time-bound.
* [SWOT Analysis](swot-analysis.md) - How to find the best solution by mapping strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
