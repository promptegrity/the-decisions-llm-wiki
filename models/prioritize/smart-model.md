# Overview

The SMART Model tests whether a goal is worth chasing in its current form. A goal that fails SMART is often not “ambitious” — it is **uncheckable**, so you cannot prioritize, learn, or stop. The model forces the goal into a shape you can act on and verify.

**SMART** usually means: **S**pecific, **M**easurable, **A**chievable (or Attainable), **R**elevant, **T**ime-bound. Variants exist; the intent is the same — turn fog into a commitment you can execute.

### Diagram

```text
  S — Specific      what exactly will be true?
  M — Measurable    how will I know?
  A — Achievable    stretch or fantasy?
  R — Relevant      why this goal?
  T — Time-bound    by when?
```

# When to use

- A goal sounds good (“get healthier,” “grow the business”) but never drives weekly choices.
- You are starting a project and need a success definition before building.
- Motivation is high but progress is invisible — you may be measuring the wrong thing.
- You need to negotiate a shared target with someone else.

Skip it when you are choosing among already-clear options (use [SWOT Analysis](/models/prioritize/swot-analysis.md) or [Yes/No Rule](/models/choose/yes-no-rule.md)), when you face a values dilemma between two goods (use [Rubber Band Model](/models/choose/rubber-band-model.md)), or when the issue is too many goals at once (use [Project Portfolio](/models/prioritize/project-portfolio.md) / [Jam Paradox](/models/choose/jam-paradox.md)).

# How it works

Rewrite the goal until each letter has a clear answer:

| Letter | Question | Weak → Stronger |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| **S**pecific | What exactly will be true? | “Get fit” → “Run a continuous 5 km without stopping” |
| **M**easurable | How will I know? | “Write more” → “Publish 4 posts / month” |
| **A**chievable | Given constraints, is this stretch or fantasy? | Cut scope or extend time if needed |
| **R**elevant | Why this goal — for which larger aim? | Drop goals that only signal virtue |
| **T**ime-bound | By when? | Add a date and an intermediate check-in |

### Steps

1. Write the goal in one sentence as you currently say it.
2. Stress-test each SMART letter; rewrite until none are hand-wavy.
3. Add a **first milestone** (often within 1–2 weeks) so the goal enters the calendar.
4. If Achievable or Relevant fails hard, change the goal — do not inflate the adjective.

### Practice tip

If you cannot fail the goal (no date, no measure), you also cannot succeed. Prefer a smaller SMART goal you can hit and then raise, over a poetic goal that never lands.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Raw goal** — State the goal in one sentence as you currently say it.
2. **SMART check** — Answer Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound for that sentence; note which letters fail.
3. **Rewrite** — Rewrite the goal until every letter has a clear answer.
4. **Milestone** — What first milestone (within 1–2 weeks) will put this goal on the calendar?
5. **Commit** — Is this the goal you will pursue, or do Achievable/Relevant force a different goal?
