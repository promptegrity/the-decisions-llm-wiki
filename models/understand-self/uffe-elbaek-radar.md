# Overview

The Uffe Elbæk Radar is a self-portrait by numbers: you rate several life dimensions (body, mind, work, relationships, and similar axes) and plot them on a **radar / spider chart**. The shape shows balance and neglect at a glance — not a personality type, but a snapshot of how you are resourcing yourself now.

Named after Danish politician and educator Uffe Elbæk; widely used as a quick personal stock-take before planning change.

### Diagram

```text
        axis1
          |
   axis6--+--axis2
         / \
     axis5  axis3
          |
        axis4
  (score each spoke 0–10)
```

# When to use

- You feel “fine” or “off” but cannot say *where*.
- You want a periodic review (monthly / quarterly) of whole-life balance.
- Coaching or journaling needs a shared visual, not only narrative.
- You are about to set goals and want them to target weak axes, not only familiar strengths.

Skip it when you need deep history (use [Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md)), social feedback calibration (use [Johari Window](/models/understand-self/johari-window.md)), or job-change timing (use [Personal Performance Model](/models/understand-self/personal-performance-model.md)).

# How it works

### Typical axes (adapt freely)

Common sets include some mix of:

- Body / health
- Mind / learning
- Work / achievement
- Relationships / family / friends
- Creativity / play
- Finances / security
- Meaning / contribution
- Environment / home

Use 6–8 axes so the chart stays readable.

### Steps

1. **Define axes** — pick dimensions that matter for *your* life, not a generic ideal.
2. **Score** — rate each from 1 (neglected / painful) to 10 (nourished / strong) *as of today*.
3. **Plot** — draw the radar; notice spikes, valleys, and overall area.
4. **Interpret** — valleys are candidates for attention; spikes may be over-invested or genuine strengths to protect.
5. **Choose one move** — one axis, one concrete experiment for the next period; re-score later.

### Questions while scoring

1. Am I scoring aspiration (“who I want to be”) or reality (“how it is”)? Stay with reality.
2. Which low score, if raised by two points, would lift several others?
3. Which high score am I defending at the expense of the rest?
4. Does this shape match how I felt last month — or am I in denial on one spoke?

### Practice tip

Re-draw the radar on the same axes over time. The *delta* teaches more than any single pretty shape. Pair a low axis with a [Flow State](/models/understand-self/flow-state.md) activity that also feeds that dimension.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Axes** — Agree 6–8 life dimensions to score (use the page defaults or adapt).
2. **Score** — Rate each axis (e.g. 0–10) for current reality — not ideal.
3. **Shape** — Where is the radar flat, spiked, or empty?
4. **Priority** — Which 1–2 axes matter most to rebalance now?
5. **Move** — One action this month that moves a priority axis without collapsing another?
