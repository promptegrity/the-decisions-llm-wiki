# Overview

The Johari Window (Joseph Luft & Harrington Ingham) is a four-pane map of what is known about you **to yourself** and **to others**. It turns vague “self-awareness” into a practical question: what should I disclose, and what feedback should I seek, so the shared picture of me gets larger and more accurate?

The aim is not total transparency. It is to shrink costly blind spots and unnecessary façades so decisions about work, trust, and growth rest on a clearer Arena.

### Diagram

```text
                 Known to self     Not known to self
              +------------------+------------------+
Known to      | OPEN             | BLIND            |
others        | (arena)          | (spot)           |
              +------------------+------------------+
Not known to  | HIDDEN           | UNKNOWN          |
others        | (façade)         |                  |
              +------------------+------------------+
```

# When to use

- Feedback surprises you (“I never knew people saw me that way”).
- You feel misunderstood, or you hide a lot and want selective openness.
- Team or partnership trust is thin; roles and styles are opaque.
- You are preparing a 360°, coaching conversation, or onboarding into a new group.

Skip it when the issue is *belief vs evidence* in your own head (use [Unthinkable Model](/models/understand-self/unthinkable-model.md)) or when you need a structured multi-axis self-score (use [Uffe Elbæk Radar](/models/understand-self/uffe-elbaek-radar.md)).

# How it works

Draw a 2×2 of knowledge:

| | **Known to others** | **Not known to others** |
|--|---------------------|-------------------------|
| **Known to self** | **Arena (open)** — shared facts, skills, style | **Façade (hidden)** — private motives, fears, history you withhold |
| **Not known to self** | **Blind spot** — habits others see; you miss | **Unknown** — untapped potential, unconscious patterns |

### How the panes move

1. **Ask for feedback** — shrink Blind spot → grow Arena (others name what you cannot see).
2. **Disclose selectively** — shrink Façade → grow Arena (you share what helps collaboration).
3. **Explore together** — Unknown can surface through experiments, reflection, or shared history work (see [Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md)).

### Questions per pane

1. **Arena** — What do we already agree is true about how I work?
2. **Blind spot** — What do trusted people notice that I dismiss or never ask about?
3. **Façade** — What am I protecting — and does hiding it still serve me?
4. **Unknown** — Where have I never tested myself?

### Practice tip

Pick one trusted person. Ask for *one* blind-spot observation and offer *one* useful disclosure. Expand the Arena in small, reversible steps — not a dump of everything private.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Focus** — Who is “self” here (you), and which relationship or group matters?
2. **Open** — What do both you and they already know and use well?
3. **Blind** — What feedback have you heard (or avoid) that might be blind spots?
4. **Hidden** — What do you keep private that, if shared carefully, would help?
5. **Move** — One disclosure or one ask for feedback to enlarge the open pane?
