# Overview

The Past–Present–Future Model asks where your **mental center of gravity** sits. Some people rehearse the past (regret, nostalgia, grievance); others live in the future (worry, ambition, fantasy); fewer inhabit the present where action and experience actually happen.

None of the three is “wrong” — memory and foresight are tools. The model flags *imbalance*: when past or future colonize attention so thoroughly that you stop deciding and feeling in the now.

### Diagram

```text
  PAST  <---  PRESENT  --->  FUTURE
  memory      attention      anticipation
  (note which pole captures most of your week)
```

# When to use

- Rumination or anxiety dominates your day.
- You plan endlessly but rarely execute — or you drift without learning from history.
- Relationships suffer because you are “not really here.”
- You want a simple language for attention hygiene before a hard choice.

Skip it when you need to *reconstruct* biography for design (use [Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md)) or when expectations about the future are the specific trap (use [Potential Trap](/models/understand-self/potential-trap.md)).

# How it works

### Three temporal postures

| Focus | Healthy use | Overuse looks like |
|-------|-------------|--------------------|
| **Past** | Learning, gratitude, continuity | Rumination, bitterness, “golden age” freeze |
| **Present** | Attention, flow, contact | Impulsivity if cut off from past/future |
| **Future** | Goals, preparation, hope | Chronic worry, deferring life “until…” |

### Quick self-check

Over a normal day, estimate percentages of mental time in each zone (they should sum to ~100%). A workable pattern often weights **present** highest, with enough past for learning and enough future for direction — not 70% replay or 70% forecast.

### Questions

1. When I am idle, where does my mind go first — yesterday or tomorrow?
2. Which conversations am I half-missing because I am elsewhere in time?
3. What decision am I postponing by living in a future that has not arrived?
4. What past story am I still arguing with as if it could still change?

### Practice tip

For one week, mark three moments daily: *Was I in past, present, or future just now?* Patterns appear fast. Pair present-heavy blocks with [Flow State](/models/understand-self/flow-state.md) work; use past deliberately via [Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md), not as ambient noise.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Week scan** — Where did your mind live most this week: past, present, or future?
2. **Evidence** — Give one concrete example of each posture in your behavior.
3. **Cost** — Which temporal bias is costing you (rumination, absence, anxiety)?
4. **Shift** — What practice pulls you toward a healthier mix?
5. **Commit** — One cue this week to return to the present (or plan the future deliberately)?
