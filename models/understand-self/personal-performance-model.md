# Overview

The Personal Performance Model helps you judge whether your current role still fits — not by salary alone, but by the relationship between **what the job demands** and **how you are growing**. Early on, challenge often exceeds skill (stretch). Later, skill may exceed challenge (plateau). When learning flattens and energy follows, it may be time to change jobs — or to redesign the one you have.

It is a diagnostic for “Should I stay?” grounded in performance dynamics, not panic or FOMO.

### Diagram

```text
  stretch/growth
       high |     sweet spot
            |    /
       low  |--/---- overload or stale
            +----------------> time in role
```

# When to use

- You feel underused, restless, or chronically overloaded in a role.
- Anniversary thinking: “Another year — still right?”
- Promotion / lateral move / exit are all on the table.
- You confuse temporary fatigue with structural misfit.

Skip it when you need whole-life balance first (use [Uffe Elbæk Radar](/models/understand-self/uffe-elbaek-radar.md)), or when the issue is prioritizing daily tasks inside a job you still want (use [Eisenhower Matrix](/models/prioritize/eisenhower-matrix.md)).

# How it works

### The fit curve (simplified)

Think of two lines over time in a role:

1. **Job challenge / complexity** — what the role asks of you
2. **Your capability / mastery** — what you can reliably deliver

| Pattern | Signal | Often means |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Challenge ≫ skill | Anxiety, mistakes, recovery lag | Need support, learning time — or role too big |
| Challenge ≈ skill | Engagement, growth | Sweet spot (near [Flow State](/models/understand-self/flow-state.md)) |
| Skill ≫ challenge | Boredom, cynicism, coasting | Redesign scope — or leave for a harder arena |

### Decision questions

1. What am I still learning here that I could not learn faster elsewhere?
2. Has my best work in this role already happened — or is a new chapter plausible?
3. Is the pain about *this manager / season* or about *the work itself*?
4. If challenge is low, can I expand the role — or is the org structure the ceiling?
5. If challenge is high, is growth available — or only burnout?

### Practice tip

Sketch the last 2–3 years: energy and learning as rising/flat/falling. Flat learning + falling energy for several quarters is a stronger exit signal than a bad week. Pair with [Rubber Band Model](/models/choose/rubber-band-model.md) if you are torn between stay and go.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Role** — Which job/role are we evaluating?
2. **Challenge vs growth** — How much stretch vs learning are you getting now?
3. **Fit** — On the simplified curve: under-challenged, sweet spot, or overload/stale?
4. **Signals** — What body/mind signals say it is time to change or stay?
5. **Decision** — Stay and redesign, negotiate stretch, or plan an exit — which, and first step?
