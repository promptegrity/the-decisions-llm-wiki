# Overview

The Making-Of Model treats your life like a film: before you write the next act, study the **making-of** — formative scenes, turning points, casts of characters, recurring motifs. You design a better future not by inventing a stranger, but by understanding how you were made: which choices compounded, which wounds still direct traffic, which strengths were rehearsed early.

Knowing the past here is strategic archaeology, not nostalgia for its own sake.

### Diagram

```text
  past scenes --> patterns --> keep/drop --> next scene (future design)
```

# When to use

- You are planning a major life redesign (career, place, relationship, identity chapter).
- Patterns keep repeating and you cannot see the script.
- CV / bio / personal brand work feels hollow without a through-line.
- Therapy-adjacent reflection: “How did I become someone who decides like this?”

Skip it when you are stuck *ruminating* without design intent (rebalance with [Past–Present–Future Model](/models/understand-self/past-present-future.md)), or when you only need a present snapshot (use [Uffe Elbæk Radar](/models/understand-self/uffe-elbaek-radar.md)).

# How it works

### Build your making-of reel

1. **Timeline** — mark 8–12 decisive scenes (moves, mentors, failures, loves, exits).
2. **Cast** — who shaped you; whose voice still narrates your choices?
3. **Motifs** — themes that recur (rescue, prove, escape, excel, hide…).
4. **Directorial choices** — where you had agency; where you defaulted.
5. **Deleted scenes** — paths not taken; what they still represent.
6. **Next act brief** — given this reel, what continuity and what break do you want?

### Questions

1. Which three scenes still explain my reflexes under stress?
2. What am I still trying to prove to someone from an old scene?
3. Which strength was rewarded early — and now limits me?
4. If this were a sequel, what must change so it is not a remake?

### Practice tip

Write the making-of in third person once (“She left because…”). Distance reveals structure. Then write the next-act brief in first person with one continuity (keep) and one discontinuity (change). Link axioms you discover to [Unthinkable Model](/models/understand-self/unthinkable-model.md).

### Facilitation steps

1. **Arc** — What future chapter are you designing for?
2. **Scenes** — List key past “making-of” scenes that shaped you (wins, wounds, turns).
3. **Patterns** — What recurring strengths or traps appear in that reel?
4. **Keep/drop** — What from the past must continue vs be left behind?
5. **Next scene** — What scene will you film next (concrete action) to author the future?
