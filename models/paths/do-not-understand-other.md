# Situation

You cannot read a person, group, or counterpart: conflict, mismatched expectations, or a system/person that stays opaque.

# Job placement

Primary: **relate**. Use **uncertainty** when the “other” is a black box or risk surface.

# Path (ordered)

1. **[Appreciative Inquiry](/models/relate/appreciative-inquiry.md)** — if the relationship can be built from strengths and desired future (not only problems).  
2. **[Conflict Resolution Model](/models/relate/conflict-resolution-model.md)** — if positions are locked; move to interests.  
3. **[Expectation Management](/models/relate/expectation-management-model.md)** — satisfaction = experience − expectation; reset promises.  
4. **[Maslow](/models/relate/maslow-pyramid.md)** or **[Bourdieu / Sinus](/models/relate/bourdieu-sinus-milieus.md)** — if motives or social world are the missing map.  
5. Opacity of a system/person → **[Black Box](/models/uncertainty/black-box-model.md)** or **[Rumsfeld](/models/uncertainty/rumsfeld-matrix.md)**.

# Alternate forks

- Pure cooperation dilemma → [Prisoner's Dilemma](/models/relate/prisoners-dilemma.md).  
- Gift / symbolic mismatch → [Gift Dilemma](/models/relate/gift-dilemma.md).

# Done when

You can state the other’s likely interests or constraints without mind-reading claims, and one next conversational or structural move is defined.
