# Situation

You wonder whether to stay, renegotiate, or leave a job/role: boredom, overload, identity mismatch, or “is this still me?”

# Job placement

Primary: **understand-self**. Then **choose** (and sometimes **prioritize** for goals/portfolio).

# Path (ordered)

1. **[Personal Performance Model](/models/understand-self/personal-performance-model.md)** — challenge vs growth fit.  
2. **[Flow State](/models/understand-self/flow-state.md)** — which activities still absorb you?  
3. **[Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md)** — what past patterns should design the next chapter?  
4. **[Hard Choice Paradox](/models/choose/hard-choice-paradox.md)** or **[Rubber Band](/models/choose/rubber-band-model.md)** — stay vs leave structure.  
5. **[SMART](/models/prioritize/smart-model.md)** — if you stay or go, make the next goal checkable.

# Alternate forks

- Potential/script trap (“I should be further”) → [Potential Trap](/models/understand-self/potential-trap.md).  
- Team is the problem, not the craft → [Team stuck](team-stuck.md) path.

# Done when

You can name fit status, a preferred path type, and one concrete next scene (conversation, application, redesign).
