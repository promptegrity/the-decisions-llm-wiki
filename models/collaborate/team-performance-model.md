# Overview

The Team Performance Model (Tuckman-style stages) says groups do not become teams by announcement. They pass through predictable phases — **forming**, **storming**, **norming**, **performing** (often plus **adjourning**) — each with different needs for leadership, clarity, and conflict. Trying to force “high performance” while people are still forming or storming creates fake harmony and hidden friction.

The model helps you **diagnose the stage** and intervene appropriately instead of panicking at normal turbulence.

### Diagram

```text
  Forming --> Storming --> Norming --> Performing --> Adjourning
     |           |            |             |              |
  clarify    surface      codify         deliver        close
  purpose    conflict     habits         / coach        / reset
```

# When to use

- A new team, project crew, or reorg cohort has just assembled.
- Conflict spikes and you wonder whether it is failure or a necessary stage.
- Performance plateaued after a good start.
- You are deciding how hands-on to be as a lead.

Skip it when you first need to know if the people mix is viable (use [Team Model](/models/collaborate/team-model.md)) or when role coverage is the missing piece (use [Roles Model](/models/collaborate/roles-model.md)).

# How it works

| Stage | What it feels like | What to do |
|-------|--------------------|------------|
| **Forming** | Polite, dependent, unclear | Clarify purpose, membership, first goals; provide structure |
| **Storming** | Conflict over power, process, priorities | Surface disagreements; coach fairness; do not fake consensus |
| **Norming** | Shared habits, trust rising | Codify ways of working; stabilize roles and feedback |
| **Performing** | Focus on results, flexible collaboration | Delegate, remove blockers, raise ambition |
| **Adjourning** | Ending or reset | Capture lessons; mark closure; re-form deliberately |

### Diagnostic questions

1. Are people still figuring out **who decides**, or already arguing about **how well** we deliver?
2. Is conflict about **relationships/roles**, or about **work quality**?
3. Do we have norms that survive stress, or only norms when things are easy?
4. Did a membership or goal change throw us **back a stage**?

### How to act by stage

1. **Forming** — over-communicate purpose and boundaries; invent early shared wins ([Result Optimization Model](/models/collaborate/result-optimization-model.md)).
2. **Storming** — lead more actively; protect psychological safety while refusing avoidance.
3. **Norming** — write down rituals, definition of done, decision rights.
4. **Performing** — shift to coaching and obstacle removal; measure outcomes.
5. **After disruption** — expect regression; re-form and re-norm without shame.

### Practice tip

Name the stage out loud with the team (“we’re storming — that’s expected”). Labeling normalizes conflict and shortens the stage.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Group** — Which team/project crew is this?
2. **Stage** — Forming, storming, norming, performing (or adjourning) — which fits the feel?
3. **Evidence** — What behaviors support that stage label?
4. **Lead** — What leadership move matches the stage (structure, surface conflict, coach…)?
5. **Next** — One ritual or conversation to advance or stabilize the stage?
