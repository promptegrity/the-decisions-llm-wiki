## Models

* [Management Styles](management-styles.md) - How to lead a team correctly by matching directive vs participative style to the situation and people’s maturity.
* [Result Optimization Model](result-optimization-model.md) - How to accumulate successes by stacking visible wins and reinforcing what already works.
* [Roles Model](roles-model.md) - How to change your point of view by seeing a team as a mix of complementary roles, not only job titles.
* [Team Model](team-model.md) - How to recognize whether your team is worth something — by checking skills, motivation, and chemistry together.
* [Team Performance Model](team-performance-model.md) - How to turn a group into a team by moving through forming, storming, norming, and performing.
