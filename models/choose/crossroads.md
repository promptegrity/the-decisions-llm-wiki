# Overview

At the Crossroads is the model for the moment after analysis — when maps are on the table and the question shrinks to **And now?** You stand at a junction: paths behind you (how you arrived), paths ahead (open, closed, glimpsed), and the fact that not choosing is also a path.

It is less a formula than a ritual of orientation: name the junction, honor what you leave, pick a direction you can walk today.

### Diagram

```text
              path A
             /
  YOU  ----+---- path B
             \
              path C (or stay)
```

# When to use

- A chapter is ending: job, relationship, place, identity role.
- You have used other models and still need a decisive stand.
- You feel paralyzed by equally plausible futures.
- You want a clean pause before momentum chooses for you.

Skip it when you have not yet classified the choice (use [Hard Choice Paradox](/models/choose/hard-choice-paradox.md) first), or when the “crossroads” is really task overload (use [Eisenhower Matrix](/models/prioritize/eisenhower-matrix.md)).

# How it works

### The junction sketch

1. **Where you stand** — one sentence: the decision that is actually live.
2. **Road behind** — 3 facts about how you got here (not the whole autobiography).
3. **Roads ahead** — list real options, including “stay” and “wait until date D.”
4. **Tolls** — what each path costs (time, identity, relationships, money).
5. **First milestone** — the smallest irreversible-enough step on the chosen road.
6. **Signpost for return** — when you will re-open the map (a date or trigger).

### Questions

1. What am I pretending is still reversible that is already over?
2. Which path am I avoiding because of fear — and which because it is truly wrong for me?
3. If I choose nothing for six months, which road do I end up on by default?
4. What would make this crossroads feel honored rather than rushed or postponed forever?

### Practice tip

Draw a literal fork. Label each road with a verb (Build, Leave, Repair, Explore…). Choose one verb for the next 30 days and one milestone you can complete this week. Use [Making-Of Model](/models/understand-self/making-of-model.md) so the choice continues your story on purpose — or deliberately breaks the remake.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Junction** — Describe the life or career junction you are standing in (why “now?”).
2. **Paths** — Name the visible paths (including “stay” and “unknown pause” if real).
3. **See** — For each path: first steps, costs, and who you become — without ranking yet.
4. **Body check** — Which path creates clarity or relief when you picture the first week?
5. **Step** — What is the next concrete step on the chosen path (not the whole future)?
