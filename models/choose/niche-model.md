# Overview

The Niche Model helps you decide **where to place a bet** — a career move, product focus, or skill investment — by looking for a *narrow gap* you can own rather than a crowded mainstream fight. A niche is not “smaller ambition”; it is a place where your edge and unmet demand overlap.

The point is to stop scattering effort across every opportunity and to choose a position where competition is weaker and fit is stronger.

# When to use

- You must pick one focus among many plausible options (role, market, specialty, side project).
- Broad competition feels exhausting and results look average everywhere.
- You want a clear “why us / why me” before investing time or money.
- You are choosing what *not* to pursue as much as what to pursue.

Skip it when you only need to prioritize today’s tasks (use [Eisenhower Matrix](/models/prioritize/eisenhower-matrix.md)) or when the goal itself is still fuzzy (use [SMART Model](/models/prioritize/smart-model.md) first).

# How it works

Map candidates on two practical questions:

1. **Demand / gap** — Is there a real need that is underserved, poorly served, or ignored by the mainstream?
2. **Fit / edge** — Do you have (or can you build) a credible advantage there — skill, access, taste, network, cost, or speed?

### Placement guide

| | **Strong fit** | **Weak fit** |
|--|----------------|--------------|
| **Clear gap** | **Bet** — prioritize; this is your niche candidate | **Learn or partner** — gap exists, but you are not the right owner yet |
| **Crowded / no gap** | **Hard fight** — only if you have an unusual edge | **Avoid** — high effort, low distinctiveness |

### Questions to stress-test a niche

1. **Who specifically** is underserved — and would they notice if you showed up?
2. **Why you** — what can you do repeatedly that generalists will not bother to do well?
3. **Why now** — is the gap temporary hype, or a durable need?
4. **What you drop** — naming the niche only counts if you stop competing everywhere else.

### Practice tip

Write one sentence: “I bet on **X** for **Y**, because **Z**.” If you cannot fill Z without buzzwords, the niche is not sharp enough yet.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Assets** — What strengths, skills, or assets are you considering betting on?
2. **Gaps** — Where is there an open gap others leave (audience, market, role)?
3. **Overlap** — Where do your strengths meet a real gap (the niche candidate)?
4. **Stress-test** — Demand, differentiation, and ability to deliver — which is weakest?
5. **Bet** — What small bet will you place to test the niche without all-in risk?
