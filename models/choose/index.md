# Choose & Commit

*Options, dilemmas, and hard trade-offs — move from stuck to a decision you can own.*

**Entry question:** How do I pick and stick with a path?

Return to the [Decision jobs](/index.md) map or the [models hub](/models/index.md).

## Models

* [Consequences Model](consequences-model.md) - Why you should make decisions early — options shrink as time and commitments accumulate.
* [At the Crossroads](crossroads.md) - And now? — pause at a life junction, see the paths, and choose the next step.
* [Hard Choice Paradox](hard-choice-paradox.md) - The four types of decisions — and why hard choices have no hidden “right” answer.
* [Jam Paradox](jam-paradox.md) - Why it is better to limit your choices so deciding stays possible and satisfying.
* [Niche Model](niche-model.md) - How to know what to bet on — find where your strengths meet a gap others leave open.
* [Purchase Decision Model](purchase-decision-model.md) - How to choose something complex (e.g. a car) by weighting criteria before comparing options.
* [Rubber Band Model](rubber-band-model.md) - What to do when facing a dilemma by naming what pulls you forward and what holds you back.
* [Stop Rule](stop-rule.md) - When to revise a decision — pre-commit the signals that mean “change course,” not “try harder.”
* [Unconscious Thought Theory](unconscious-thought-theory.md) - How to decide intuitively — gather facts, then let the mind work without forced analysis.
* [Yes/No Rule](yes-no-rule.md) - How to make a decision quickly with a clear default instead of endless deliberation.
