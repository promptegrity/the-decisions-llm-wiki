# Overview

**Unconscious Thought Theory (UTT)** suggests a disciplined way to decide **intuitively**: for complex choices with many attributes, conscious deliberation can over-weight what is easy to verbalize, while a period of distraction after taking in the facts can yield a better holistic judgment.

The slogan is not “go with your gut blindly.” It is: *inform the unconscious, then stop grinding*.

Associated with work by Ap Dijksterhuis and colleagues; also common in practical decision guides.

### Diagram

```text
  gather facts → CONSCIOUS pause (distract) → return with holistic pick → quick deal-breaker check
```

# When to use

- The choice has many factors (job offer, apartment, strategy option, complex purchase).
- Spreadsheet thinking leaves you more confused the longer you stare.
- You have already collected the important information and comparisons.
- You can afford a short incubation period (hours to a day or two), not a month of avoidance.

Skip it for simple, rule-based decisions (use [Yes/No Rule](/models/choose/yes-no-rule.md)), when you still lack basic facts (research first), or when timing risk dominates (use [Consequences Model](/models/choose/consequences-model.md)).

# How it works

### Recommended sequence

1. **Absorb** — gather the relevant options and attributes; write them down so nothing critical is forgotten.
2. **Stop analyzing** — deliberately switch to an unrelated activity (walk, chores, light work). Do not keep “just one more” pro/con pass.
3. **Return and decide** — notice which option feels clearer or more stable; choose without reopening endless debate.
4. **Quick conscious check** — confirm no hard constraint was ignored (budget, ethics, deadline, non-negotiable need).

### Simple vs complex

| Situation | Prefer |
|-----------|--------|
| Few clear criteria, right/wrong rules | Conscious, explicit reasoning |
| Many soft trade-offs, hard to weigh precisely | Unconscious incubation after briefing yourself |
| Safety-critical or irreversible with sparse data | Slow down; seek more information or expert rules — do not romanticize gut |

### Practice tip

Set a timer: “I will research until 3pm, then leave it alone until tomorrow morning, then decide.” Intuition here is a *scheduled* step, not a mood. If you skip the absorb phase, “intuition” is just bias.

### Facilitation steps

1. **Complexity** — Is this simple (few attributes) or complex (many factors hard to verbalize)?
2. **Inform** — What facts have you already gathered? List them without deciding yet.
3. **Distract** — Agree on a pause (walk, sleep, unrelated task) without forced pro/con grinding.
4. **Return** — After the pause, what does the holistic preference feel like?
5. **Check** — Does a quick conscious check find a deal-breaker, or do you commit?
