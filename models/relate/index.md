# Relate & Negotiate

*Other people as partners, opponents, or gifts — motives, conflict, trust, expectations.*

**Entry question:** How do we understand each other and the relationship?

Return to the [Decision jobs](/index.md) map or the [models hub](/models/index.md).

## Models

* [Appreciative Inquiry](appreciative-inquiry.md) - What kind of interlocutor are you? — an appreciative approach that builds on strengths and desired futures.
* [Bourdieu and Sinus-Milieus](bourdieu-sinus-milieus.md) - Where is your place? — social position, capital, and lifestyle milieus that shape taste and behavior.
* [Conflict Resolution Model](conflict-resolution-model.md) - How to resolve a conflict with elegance — positions, interests, and paths out of escalation.
* [Expectation Management Model](expectation-management-model.md) - If you are looking for a soulmate — be careful — satisfaction is expectation minus experience.
* [Gift Dilemma](gift-dilemma.md) - What to give and to whom — match the gift to the relationship, not only to the occasion.
* [Maslow's Pyramid](maslow-pyramid.md) - What you really need versus what you really want — a hierarchy of human motives.
* [Prisoner's Dilemma](prisoners-dilemma.md) - When trust is profitable — cooperation vs defection when each side’s best individual move can make everyone worse off.
